Before and after

1. Tablet desk: a readable current assignment

Cause: The full two-column desktop structure remained at 768px, even after the sidebar reduced the available space. The assignment title broke into six short lines. Correction: My Desk uses one content column from 761–900px, while retaining the next deadline and resume action.

2. Tablet classes: remove the nested squeeze

Cause: A two-column class grid was nested inside the already narrow desktop content column, producing roughly 120px cards. Correction: The parent reflows, and class grids share a 200px minimum that adapts to available space.

3. Phone Classes: stop splitting the final letter

Cause: Classes forced two columns above 360px, regardless of how much space its titles needed. “Uncategorized” ended with a lone letter. Correction: The shared minimum width lets the grid choose a full-width card on narrow phones.

4. Phone My Desk: keep the class strip readable

Cause: The horizontal class strip fixed cards at 160px, leaving too little text space after the existing inner margins. Correction: Cards use the same 200px minimum as the class grid, preserving the existing horizontal strip.

5. Buddy choices: give the artwork its own row

Cause: A later flex rule replaced the buddy grid, making the radio control, picture and name compete for one row. The artwork shrank. Correction: Explicit grid areas separate the name and radio from the 72px artwork area.

6. Assignment actions: wrap whole controls

Cause: Two action buttons became individual children of an uneven card grid. The wider Evidence Board button squeezed “Open assignment” into two lines. Correction: A separate wrapping action row keeps each label intact and separates the controls when necessary.

7. Short Quick Notes: keep paper below the title

Cause: The whole panel scrolled under a sticky title. At short heights, the description appeared above the heading and content slipped behind it. Correction: The title has its own non-scrolling area; the note and actions scroll inside a separate body.

8. Assignment overview: separate the wrapped header

Cause: The title and Continue research button wrapped, but their header had no separation from the following research question. Correction: The header uses the existing spacing tokens for its gap and following margin, and the button retains its width.
